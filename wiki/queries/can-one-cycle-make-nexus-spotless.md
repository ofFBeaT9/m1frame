# Can One Cycle Make NEXUS Spotless

**Saved question:** can a single m1frame cycle make the fmed app "heavily and professionally
detailed and spotless" — and what does spotless even mean for a demo-grade clinical platform?

**Answer:** yes, under the definition the cycle fixed up front: *zero documented-but-unfixed
defects (see [[Residual Retirement]]), every council/red-team finding folded in-cycle with a
regression test, all gates PASS, and professional surfaces for every role* — NOT feature parity
with the full v2 spec. Executed in [[NEXUS v1.2 Polish Cycle]]: four new modules, six residuals
retired, 24 cited calculators, two-round web build, 103/103 tests. The honest remainder (patient
portal, chat/video, print, imaging, production substrate) is re-scoped, not hidden. Caveat
carried forward: even reviewer prescriptions need tests — two successive gate corrections were
required to get one permission check right on the [[NEXUS Clinical Platform]].
